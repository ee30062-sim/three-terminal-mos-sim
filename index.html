What to notice
  1. (a)→(b): raising VGB lowers the barrier from n+; electrons flow in and the surface inverts.
  2. (b)→(c): VCB lowers Ec of n+ by eVCB; the barrier grows and the inversion layer thins.
  3. (c)→(d): raising φs by VCB restores the same barrier and the same nsurface.
  4. In the plots every curve moves right with VCB; on the deep-depletion line VCB has no effect.
  5. Common-C: holding VGC and raising VCB reduces |Q′inv| (the body effect).
